How it works

Set up once. Protected everywhere.

No apps to install. No per-device configuration. DNS filtering works at the network level.

01
Create an account
Sign up for free. No credit card needed. Takes about 30 seconds.
02
Change one setting
Open your phone or computer's network settings. Enter the DNS address we give you. That's it.
03
Ads and trackers are blocked
Every website request goes through Toran DNS first. Bad stuff gets blocked before it reaches you.
04
See what's happening
Your dashboard shows what's being blocked in real time. View reports and adjust settings anytime.
